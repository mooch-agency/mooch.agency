--shadow-border / --shadow-border-hover from tokens.css. A 1px ring plus two soft lift layers, for cards that fake depth with a solid border. Used on prompt-page cards.

.disclosure / .disclosure-toggle from ui.css. Collapses to --disclosure-max, fades the cut edge via --disclosure-bg. The clamp is gated behind .is-collapsible, added by the toggle's click handler (copied per-page, see /prompts/deslop), so with no JS the content renders in full rather than being clipped behind a toggle that never appears. The handler releases the measured height once the open animation ends, so a later resize can't re-clip it, and print always expands.
