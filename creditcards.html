§ 01The collection

Credits is Jack Butcher's work, and this page is a fan index, not an official one. Each Credit began as one $8 payment through X Money. The transaction ID, hashed with SHA-256, draws four 8x8 grids in cyan, magenta, yellow and black, so the receipt is the artwork. 122,154 payments made it in before the window closed.

From 1 October 2026, any 80 Credits can be burned to assemble one Statement. At most 1,526 Statements can ever exist, and every one made shrinks the supply for good.

Start at jack.art/credits. Trade on OpenSea. Follow @jackbutcher. Verify the contract.
