What are Credits?

Credits is an open edition by Jack Butcher. Each $8 payment through X Money minted one Credit, and its transaction ID, hashed with SHA-256, draws the artwork: four 8x8 grids in cyan, magenta, yellow and black.

From 1 October 2026, any 80 Credits can be burned to make one Statement. Every burn shrinks the supply for good.
